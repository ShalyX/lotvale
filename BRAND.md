# Identity decisions

Name: Lotvale. Descriptor: purchase planning. Promise line: Make the whole order work.

Naming explored: literal (Basketplan, Quote Ledger, Order-ready), allocation (Lotform, Pairlot, Lotvale), stability (Sumstead, Ternsum, Cartmere), transformation (Buyfold, Ordergrain). Lotform, Pairlot, Sumstead and Buyfold had direct product/company collisions; Ordergrain has historical company/code usage. Lotvale had no obvious software collision in the targeted search on 2026-11-13; this is not trademark clearance. Pronunciation: LOT-vayl. Avoid ambiguous spelling stylization.

Concept territories: (1) a packing arrangement, representing complete fit, with a generic box risk; (2) ruled quote sheets merging at a shared order edge, representing comparison becoming action; (3) a purchasing tally, with generic finance-logo risk. Choose territory 2. Mark: three unequal horizontal strips with a shared right edge and a cut-in lower strip. Preserve the distinctive length sequence and inset; no enclosing box, arrow, shield, shopping cart or AI sparkle.

Palettes considered: industrial cobalt/yellow, ink/oxide/stone, and plum/chalk/copper. Choose oxide wine #792c42, carbon #26272b, cool chalk #f5f5f2, dusty rose #eadde0, muted sage #e5ece4 for confirmed status. No gradient. Source sheets, pack units and actual allocations carry the visual language.

Type: strong grotesk-style system sans for headings and controls, tabular figures for prices, compact uppercase document labels. Avoid fake enterprise claims and generic AI promises. Voice: practical and specific. Assets: monochrome SVG mark, favicon, social cover generated from the same ruled-strip system; exact text in SVG.

Ownability critique: strip marks can be generic, so the stepped 3:2:4 length relationship and offset are used consistently. The mark alone does not imply procurement; the document grid and allocation language give it context. It works at 16px and in black. No trademark or worldwide visual-uniqueness claim.
