# Lotvale — Before you place the order

STATUS: APPROVED BY USER FOR PRODUCTION. The user instructed: build the two-minute demo from this script and storyboard, with natural connected voiceover, engaging motion and a clear real SERV flow. See PRODUCTION.md for measured timing and execution evidence. Narration wording below remains unchanged.

Creative target: approximately two minutes at a natural explanatory pace. Timing below is an editorial guide, not a hackathon limit. The recorded read should determine final shot lengths. New connected narration; do not reuse the disconnected one-line voice stems.

Audience takeaway: Lotvale helps a small-business buyer turn supplier quotes into a complete purchase that meets their budget and deadline. SERV Reasoning interprets supplier language; the buyer checks the terms; Lotvale calculates complete plans and prepares supplier drafts.

## Full narration

### 0:00–0:16 — A buyer with a real decision

Imagine you're opening a café. You need cups, lids, sleeves and napkins delivered before opening day. You have an eight-hundred-and-fifty-dollar budget, and three suppliers have sent their quotes.

### 0:16–0:29 — Why the cheapest quote is not enough

One has the lowest pack prices, but arrives too late. The others have different pack sizes, delivery charges and minimum orders. Now you have to work out which complete order you can actually buy.

### 0:29–0:41 — Introduce Lotvale through its purpose

That's what Lotvale helps you do. Start with what you need, your budget and your deadline. Then bring in the supplier quotes. Here's the quote from Counter Supply.

### 0:41–1:00 — SERV does the interpretation

When we ask Lotvale to interpret it, the supplier text goes to SERV Reasoning. SERV matches the quoted products to our basket and extracts the pack sizes, prices, freight, tax and dates into an editable draft. We can inspect those terms in the workspace.

### 1:00–1:18 — Make the review meaningful

We check the draft against the original quote: a hundred cups per pack, twenty-eight dollars for freight, and delivery on November nineteenth. We review the remaining items and charges, then confirm the terms. SERV handles the interpretation; the buying decision stays with us.

### 1:18–1:40 — Show the complete purchase payoff

Lotvale can now compare complete buying plans, accounting for packs, minimums, freight, tax and delivery. Here, the lowest complete on-time plan is seven hundred and fifty-five dollars and eight cents. It covers all four items, fits the budget, and arrives two days before the deadline.

### 1:40–1:54 — Carry the decision into useful work

From that plan, we prepare the matching supplier draft, with the quantities and full order total. The purchase can also be saved to a business account, ready to pick up later. Nothing is ordered automatically.

### 1:54–2:00 — Close on the value

Lotvale. Understand the whole purchase before you place the order.

## Production notes for the next stage

- The narration is one connected explanation. Record a full natural read before locking frame counts. Keep breathing room at the returned terms, confirmation and completed plan.
- Motion is an engagement tool: moving documents, a clear date conflict, camera direction through actual fields, and a strong complete-order reveal. Avoid repeating the same left-heading/right-screen layout through every scene.
- SERV receives roughly37 seconds of direct interpretation/review coverage and is named at the point where its contribution is visible. Its role is specific: quote language into matched, editable commercial terms. Lotvale's planner calculates the financial result.
- The café and suppliers are fictional, consistently labeled. No customer traction, measured savings, accuracy percentage or processing-speed promise is made.
- Keep quantities, quote reference, dates, freight, tax and755.08 consistent from input to output. Never cut between different purchases as though they were one run.
- The last60-second cut is rejected as the narrative source. Existing footage may be reused only where it supports this approved story and its exact visible states are checked. New voiceover is required.
- Before any render: user reviews this script and storyboard. Then record the narration, measure its actual pace, capture missing shots, build the edit and review it from beginning to end with sound.
